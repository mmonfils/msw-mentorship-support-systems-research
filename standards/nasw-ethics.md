# NASW Code of Ethics Standards Alignment

Structured mentorship directly supports compliance with professional social work ethics by establishing clear boundaries, promoting competence, and protecting client welfare through supported practice.

## Core Ethical Standards

### Standard 1.01: Commitment to Clients
* **Principle**: Social workers' primary responsibility is to promote the well-being of clients.
* **Mentorship Application**: Well-supported students and supervisees provide higher quality, ethically sound services to clients, mitigating early-career burnout and burnout-induced harm.

### Standard 1.05: Cultural Competence
* **Principle**: Social workers should demonstrate understanding of culture and its function in human behavior and society.
* **Mentorship Application**: Mentors assist mentees in navigating cultural humility, recognizing personal biases, and serving diverse client populations ethically.

### Standard 2.05: Consultation
* **Principle**: Social workers should seek the advice and counsel of colleagues whenever such consultation is in the best interests of clients.
* **Mentorship Application**: Structured mentorship normalizes seeking consultation early and often, dismantling the myth of individual practitioner omniscience.

### Standard 3.02: Education and Training
* **Principle**: Social workers who function as educators, field instructors, or trainers should provide instruction only within their areas of knowledge and competence.
* **Mentorship Application**: Frameworks establish clear role expectations and training requirements for field instructors and peer mentors.
