# Standards Alignment Matrix

This matrix details how the MSW Mentorship framework aligns with the Council on Social Work Education (CSWE) 2022 Educational Policy and Accreditation Standards (EPAS) and the NASW Code of Ethics.

## CSWE 2022 EPAS Competencies

### Competency 1: Demonstrate Ethical and Professional Behavior
* **Application**: Mentorship is framed as a professional duty rather than an informal luxury. Structured mentorship equips early career practitioners to navigate ethical dilemmas, authority dynamics, and professional boundaries.
* **Workshop Objective**: Shift mentorship from reactive crisis management to proactive professional development.

### Competency 2: Advance Human Rights and Social, Economic, and Environmental Justice
* **Application**: Applying Freirean critical pedagogy to dismantle "banking model" supervision dynamics. Emphasizes equity for non-traditional, first-generation, and remote graduate students.
* **Workshop Objective**: Identify micro, mezzo, and macro institutional barriers to student persistence.

### Competency 9: Evaluate Practice with Individuals, Families, Groups, Organizations, and Communities
* **Application**: Continuous evaluation of mentorship access metrics within graduate programs to improve retention and student well-being.
* **Workshop Objective**: Synthesize live participant input to inform departmental advising models.
