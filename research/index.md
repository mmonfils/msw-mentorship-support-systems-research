## Key Research Takeaways

1. **Systemic Isolation**: Non-traditional and remote MSW students encounter significant institutional barriers due to rigid advising schedules and uncompensated field hours.
2. **Near-Peer Efficacy**: Advanced MSW and doctoral mentorship creates accessible, low-stakes spaces for navigating professional identity and ethical dilemmas.
3. **Structured Praxis**: Transitioning from informal mentorship to institutionalized frameworks directly correlates with higher post-graduation professional retention.
