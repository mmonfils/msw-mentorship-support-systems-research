# Theoretical Frameworks

Our mentorship models are grounded in established social work and educational theories to ensure interventions address both individual development and systemic institutional reform.

## Core Theories

* **[Critical Pedagogy](./critical-pedagogy.md)**: Applying Paulo Freire's critique of "banking model" education to social work field supervision and advising.
* **[Systems Theory](./systems-theory.md)**: Analyzing student persistence and institutional friction through Person-in-Environment (PIE) and macro-level structural lenses.
