### 2. Confidentiality Protection Protocols
To ensure adherence to federal Common Rule guidelines and protect participant security, the following measures are enforced within this public archive:
* **Decoupled Identity Matrix:** Direct identifiers (names, emails, contact coordinates) collected on informed consent records are physically separated from raw narrative files.
* **Aggregated Profile Tracking:** Only group tracking variables are evaluated. Individual narratives are referenced strictly through unique identifiers (**Participant A** through **Participant E**).
* **Omission of Raw Transcripts:** Appendix D (Full Uncut Audio/Video Interview Transcripts) is permanently excluded from this open-access structure to avoid accidental identity disclosure via contextual local tracking clues.

## 📊 Summary of Qualitative Themes & Empirical Scope

This research project explores the role of mentorship within Master of Social Work (MSW) structures through the theoretical framework of **Critical Pedagogy** (Freirean dialogue). Between five and eight semi-structured interviews were deployed to examine lived experiences regarding access barriers.

```
  - Structural Mentorship Alignment                 - Countering the "Fishbowl Effect"
```

### Core Extracted Themes:
1. **The Online Student Experience:** Highlighting the sharp dynamic between structural isolation and the need for immediate peer connection.
2. **Faculty Accessibility Footprint:** Documenting that students prioritize faculty contact as a vital source of developmental, psychosocial mentoring rather than simple administrative task checking.
3. **Identity Matrix Balancing:** Evaluating the minimal margin of error available to non-traditional graduate students managing employment, parenting duties, and accelerated academic loads simultaneously.
