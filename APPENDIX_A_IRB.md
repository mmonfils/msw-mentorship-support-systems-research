### 🔬 Project Operational Parameters

#### Brief Description of the Project
Graduate research course project exploring the concept of mentorship and its role in the experiences of Master of Social Work students. Established mentoring approaches and programs will be examined, and the existing literature on the subject will be analyzed within the theoretical framework of critical pedagogy. Between five and eight interviews will be conducted with social work graduate students to understand lived experiences related to the research topic.

### 🛡️ Risk Mitigation & Storage Protocols

#### Risk Minimization Measures
Names on informed consent forms are separated from the data files. Names or any other personally identifiable elements are strictly excluded from the written analysis of the research data. Public presentation of materials (such as the NASW-Iowa Fall Symposium or GV Rise Research Showcase) will be conducted using aggregated metrics or character-driven identification labels to protect anonymity.

#### Data Preservation & Security Methods
All raw research data files, transcription texts, and analytical spreadsheets are stored exclusively on a secure, password-protected computer system. No connection link between direct identifiers and code numbers is retained following data collection processing.
