### 🎯 Introduction & Target Eligibility
You are being invited to participate in a research study exploring the role of mentorship within Master of Social Work (MSW) academic ecosystems. This study examines student perspectives to inform the design and deployment of proactive support frameworks. 

**Exclusion Criteria:** You are not eligible to participate if you are under 18 years of age, incarcerated, cognitively disabled, an elected official, an appointed public official, a political candidate, or currently enrolled in the same research course sections as the principal investigator (SWGR 585 Research-Informed Practice II at Grand View University).

---

### 📋 Operational Commitments & Disclosures

#### 1. What will my participation involve?
If you agree to participate, you will complete an interview regarding your journey, needs, and observations as an MSW student. The interview format will last approximately 45 minutes and will be recorded via audio/video tools to guarantee reporting accuracy. Sessions will take place at Grand View University or a secure, public meeting alternative (e.g., a library conference room).

#### 2. Are there any risks to me?
There are no foreseeable risks to you greater than what you would encounter in everyday life. If you experience negative impacts during or after this study, you are advised to immediately contact the course instructor (Dr. Cathy Beck-Cross) and the Grand View IRB Chair (Dr. Bryan McQuide, bmcquide@grandview.edu, 515-263-2993).

#### 3. Are there any benefits or compensation?
There are no direct benefits or financial compensations for participating. Your involvement contributes to the broader data landscape tracking unmet student needs, helping to shape alternative future institutional support designs.

#### 4. Ability to Withdraw From the Research
Your participation is completely voluntary. You retain the right to refuse to answer any specific query while remaining in the study. The investigator may stop the research interaction at any point if it is deemed in your best clinical or academic interest.

---

### 🔒 Confidentiality & Public Presentation Rules

* **Academic Use Limits:** This study is conducted as part of graduate coursework. Results are used for academic and professional reporting purposes only.
* **Data Security Lifespan:** Audio and video records used to generate typed transcripts will be secured for a mandatory five-year retention window before complete electronic destruction.
* **Public Forums & Symposia:** If selected for external distribution (e.g., the GV Rise research symposium or workplace professional panels), data files will be completely aggregated. Direct interview quotes will only be introduced using unique research identification labels, letters, or pseudonyms.
