# Appendix C: Interview Guide

## Research Project Interview Protocol (Semi-Structured Guide)

### 📋 Phase 1: Operational Checklist & Greeting
1. **Greet** the participant by name and verify structural comfort in the interview environment.
2. **Introduce** yourself and outline your role as a graduate social work researcher.
3. **Explain the purpose** of the interview session: To explore how mentorship operates within the MSW academic program layout, revealing structural insights that describe how students perceive their access to supportive ecosystems.
4. **Explain information utilization parameters:** Data collected completes the final research capstone requirement. Findings identify unaddressed system needs to suggest how future formal mentorship networks can be integrated into social work training. No tracking info or identities will be disclosed.
5. **Execute Informed Consent collection:** Explain the document parameters explicitly. Ensure the participant checks the audio recording authorization box and signs two copies (one retained by the investigator, one kept by the participant). Query: *"Do you have any questions about the contents of the Informed Consent Form?"*
6. **Establish process transparency:** Inform the participant that a series of five open-ended narrative prompts will follow. Query: *"Before I activate the recording device and begin, do you have any questions?"*

---

### 💬 Phase 2: Core Qualitative Query Matrix

#### Question A: The Baseline Journey
Reflecting on your experience as a graduate student here since you first enrolled, what has your journey looked like, especially in moments where you felt you needed professional or academic guidance?

#### Question B: Independent Navigation & Autonomy
Thinking about times when you have navigated challenges independently, how would you describe a specific example of a time you felt you were “on your own” with a professional decision?

#### Question C: Roadblocks & Resource Hierarchy
When you hit a roadblock in navigating challenges such as career planning, who is the first person you think to reach out to, and why?

#### Question D: Institutional Impact on Confidence
Considering the sense of mentorship (or lack thereof) you have experienced as an MSW student, how does the current system affect your confidence in finishing the program?

#### Question E: Background & Meaningful Relationships
When you think about “meaningful relationships” in the academic setting, what does that look like for someone coming from your specific background?

---

### 📋 Phase 3: Closing Protocol
7. **Thank the participant** for their time and contribution to social work knowledge generation: *"Thank you for helping to make this research possible by sharing your thoughts and experiences. Do you have any final questions before we conclude?"*
8. **Provide the participant** with their physical copy of the executed Informed Consent document before departure.
