### ⚙️ Analytical Ground Rules
1. **Broad Scan:** Read through all transcription records continuously to establish a holistic baseline sense of the participant responses.
2. **Pattern Tracking:** Re-read the logs carefully, documenting margin notes where repetitions or semantic shifts surface.
3. **Nuance Filtering:** Evaluate text strings a third time to isolate subtle contradictions, structural conditions, or emotional stressors.
4. **Keyword Extraction:** Populate the tracking grids below with raw text patterns extracted from the primary participant records.
